Employees & Duties

Duty Groups

Configure your duty groups here - drag the ☰ handle to reorder groups, and drag duties/employees onto the slots. Switch to the Duty Sheet tab to see and print the resulting day-off calendar.

Skills Matrix

Mark which duties/rounds each employee is trained and signed off to cover. When someone's on annual leave, sick, or otherwise absent on a working day, the Duty Sheet suggests a skilled, available person to cover their round. Import expects "Employee" and "Duty" column headers, matched by name - export first to see the exact format.

Uncovered Duties

Any working day with nobody actually covering it - no one assigned, or the assigned person is on annual leave, sick, or another absence with no cover arranged yet. Grouped by day, for whichever weeks/groups are currently shown. Click an entry to jump to it.

Who's Off

Everyone booked as Annual Leave, Sick, on another absence, or simply on their normal day off, for whichever weeks/groups are currently shown - absences shown here even once cover has been arranged for them. Click an entry to jump to it.

Annual Leave & Absence

Each employee's booked annual-leave weeks, with a running count. Import from an Annual Leave Plan workbook (it will ask you to confirm any names that don't match exactly), a clean "Employee"/"Week Commencing" export of your own, or add/remove individual weeks by hand below.

Day-level sickness or other absence, separate from booked annual leave - shown in its own colour on the Duty Sheet, and used to suggest a skilled cover for any working day missed.

Hours & Overtime

Each employee's normal daily start/end time, Monday to Saturday (nobody works Sunday). Hours are calculated automatically and used to work out overtime when someone covers a duty on their day off.

Automatically detected whenever a manual entry on the Duty Sheet shows someone working (green/blue/purple) on a day their rotation says should be off. Set an employee's daily times above for their hours to calculate correctly.

Bank / Public Holidays

Dates added here show automatically as BH in a distinct colour on the Duty Sheet calendar, for every role that day, overriding the normal rotation.

Fiscal Year Week Numbering

Sets Week 1 of the fiscal year, used to show a week number (e.g. "Week 22") in the Overtime Log so entries are easier to track and match against payroll. Royal Mail's fiscal year typically starts on a Monday in late March - update this each year when the new fiscal year begins.

Password Lock

There is no way to recover this password if it's forgotten. The data is genuinely encrypted with it - write the password down somewhere safe before setting it.

Use the Lock now button in the top-right header to lock this system immediately without closing the browser - handy before stepping away from a shared computer.

The system locks itself automatically after this long with no mouse, keyboard, or scroll activity - so an unattended unlocked screen isn't left open indefinitely.

This protects the data stored in this browser (and casual access to this screen). Export backup always produces an encrypted file, whether or not a password is set here - if none is set, it asks you to choose one just for that backup.

Anonymised Demo Copy

Makes a separate copy of your data for showing the tool to management or anyone else - every employee name, duty/round name, and office name is replaced with a realistic sample value, so nobody's real details are in it. Everything else (the rotation patterns, leave weeks, overtime logic, colours - the actual functionality) stays fully intact and demonstrable.

This only affects the downloaded file - your data here is not changed.

Modules

Core features are always on. Optional features can be switched off if your office doesn't use them - their data is never deleted, so turning one back on later restores full access to whatever was already there. Changing a toggle reloads the page.

Rota Sheet

One row per person, colour-coded across the week - days off, annual leave, sickness, and the duty they're covering - plus a plain-language list of what still needs covering underneath, with a suggestion for who to ask.

Rotation Patterns

Two built-in Royal Mail patterns are ready to use. Duplicate one to build a custom variant, or create a brand new pattern from scratch. In the schedule grid, just type into a cell - a single letter on a Saturday goes blue (Saturday Route), typing another role's name goes purple (Covering), "OFF" goes yellow, and anything else stays green (Working). Click the small dot in the corner any time to set the colour manually instead.

Edit pattern

This is a built-in pattern - you can rename it above, but the day-off grid itself is locked. Click Duplicate on the card to create an editable copy with its own schedule.
